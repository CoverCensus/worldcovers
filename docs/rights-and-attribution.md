# Rights and Attribution

Who may use what in the APMC, how to credit it, and what to do when something is unclear. This page reports what the APMC states today. It does not change any terms, and where a term has not been confirmed it says so in the last section. Terms such as Citation, Contributor, Entry and Reference Work are defined in the **Glossary** in Help.

---

## Two different kinds of rights

**The software.** The application behind the APMC (WorldCovers) is free software, published under the GNU General Public License, version 3. That license covers the program, not the records it holds.

**The catalog data.** Every page of this site states: "All catalog data is freely available under CC BY 4.0." That is the statement the APMC makes about the records it publishes. In general, CC BY 4.0 lets anyone copy, share and build on licensed material, including commercially, as long as they give credit, link to the license and say whether they changed anything. The license text is at https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/. Which parts of a record that statement reaches, and whether it reaches images, are open questions listed under "Not yet confirmed".

## Where the records come from

The APMC holds listings from published reference works and from its Contributors. The main sources so far are the *American Stampless Cover Catalog* (5th and 6th editions), the *Virginia Postal History Catalog* (1st edition) and *Florida Postal History 1763-1861* (2nd edition), as listed on the Reference Works page. Every record that came from a catalog shows that catalog as a Citation, with a page or section where one is known. A listing taken from a catalog is accepted as genuine on that catalog's authority.

The American Postal Markings Catalog is a project of the U.S. Philatelic Classics Society, which entrusted the WorldCovers software project with delivering it. The printed catalogs remain the work of their editors and publishers, who are named on the **Acknowledgements** page. The APMC has not confirmed the terms for reusing a catalog's original text, layout or page images beyond this site; ask the publisher first.

## Images

Images are added by Contributors and Editors when they submit or edit a record. A record names the person who submitted it only when that person chose to be named (see the next section). The APMC does not publish a separate photographer or rights holder for an image. The rights in an image depend on who scanned or photographed it, and the record may not say who that was.

If you want to reuse an image beyond this site, ask first (see "Corrections and questions"). Whether the CC BY 4.0 statement reaches images has not been confirmed.

If you upload an image, only upload one you have the right to share. If you photographed or scanned a cover that someone else owns, say so in the description, and name the owner if they want to be named.

## Credit for Contributors

When you submit a record you can check the box "Would you like your name to display as the submitter?". If you check it, the record shows "Submitted by" with your name. If you leave it unchecked, no name is shown on the record.

Only you, the original Contributor, can change that choice: sign in, open the record, submit an edit and change the box. Editors and Administrators cannot change it for you.

Today the credit names the person who first submitted the record. People who later edit a record are not credited on it. That is a known gap, listed under "Not yet confirmed".

## What to keep in mind when you submit

Submitting is a proposal: an Editor reviews it before anything is published. A few habits make a submission easy to trust:

- Submit only material you have the right to share.
- When a listing comes from a publication, cite it in the Reference Works section of the form, with the page or section.
- When a cover or image belongs to someone else, say so in the description.
- Decide whether you want your name shown, using the box described above.

## Reusing records: how to credit the APMC

Creative Commons recommends four elements in a credit: the title, the author, the source and the license, plus a note of any changes. Here is that pattern for the two kinds of material you might take from this site.

In both examples, replace [record number] with the address shown in your browser for that record.

**A catalog record (data).** Suppose you copy a Marking's details into an article and reformat its dates.

> Marking ASCC6-VA-M0001, Richmond, Va. townmark. American Postal Markings Catalog (APMC), U.S. Philatelic Classics Society, https://hellowoco.app/record/[record number]. License: CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/, as stated by the APMC. Changes: dates rewritten as day-month-year; converted to a table.

If the record also cites a printed catalog, keep that Citation in your credit, for example "after the American Stampless Cover Catalog, 6th edition, page 123", so your readers can find the original listing.

**An image.** Suppose you crop a cover scan from a record for a talk.

> Cover from Petersburg, Va., 1851 (image). Submitted to the APMC by [the name shown on the record, or "an APMC contributor" when none is shown]. https://hellowoco.app/record/[record number]. License: not stated by the APMC; used with the rights holder's permission (ask first, see below). Changes: cropped from the original scan.

When something is unknown, give what you have. A credit with no title, or with no name because the Contributor chose not to be named, is still a correct credit.

## Corrections and questions

- **A wrong or missing source on a record:** sign in and submit a correction through the contribution workflow, adding the Reference Work and page. That goes straight to the Editors.
- **Your name is shown and you do not want it, or it is missing and you do:** sign in, open the record, submit an edit and change the "display as the submitter" box. Only you can do this.
- **An image you hold the rights to, or a rights question about any record or image:** write to info@worldcovers.org, the address in the footer of every page, with the record's address and what you are asking. Editors review such requests and can remove an image.
- **An error in this page:** see "How do I report an error" in the **FAQ**.

## Not yet confirmed

The APMC is a volunteer project, and some terms have not been written down yet. Until they are, this page does not claim them. Treat them as "ask first":

- Whether the CC BY 4.0 statement applies to listings taken from each printed catalog, or only to the fields the APMC's own Contributors and Editors have added.
- Whether the CC BY 4.0 statement reaches the images on the site.
- The terms for reusing a source catalog's original text or page images beyond this site.
- A public record of who made each image and who holds its rights; the site does not show one today.
- Credit for later editors of a record, in addition to the first Contributor.

This page will be updated when those are settled. Nothing here grants a right that the owner of the material has not granted.
